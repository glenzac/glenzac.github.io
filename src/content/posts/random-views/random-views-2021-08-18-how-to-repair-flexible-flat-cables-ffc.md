## 1\. Use Conductive Paint

Conductive paint is often a silver paste that can be applied on surfaces to create a connection.

### ✅ Pros:

- Probably the first thing to try
- Does not damage any existing component
- Least risky

### ❌Cons:

- Quite expensive due to the use of precious metals
