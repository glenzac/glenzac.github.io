I was browsing [Behance](http://www.behance.net) when I came across this beautiful and free typeface that drew inspiration from the architecture of a French college building. What took my attention was the beautifully crafted lower case 'g' and the use of negative space. I instantly decided to use this to make my text logo - the 'glenzac' you see above.

* * *

College is a free elegant stencil font from [Corentin Riviere](https://www.behance.net/corentinrvr). It began as an experimental project as a tribute to the College de France and is now a fully functioning classically styled font.  
It's letterforms were inspired by the arches & columns of the buildings at the College de France and helped create the overall feel for this project.  
Corentin is accepting donations for this project [Here](https://gumroad.com/l/rbXtz)
