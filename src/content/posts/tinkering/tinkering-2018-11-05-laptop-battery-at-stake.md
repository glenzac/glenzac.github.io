title: Laptop Battery at stake!

Pretty big a battery compared to the paltry 2200mAh batteries that come with Lenovo laptops like mine :(

So this is from an old MSI laptop that stopped working (all my attempts at bringing it to life also failed).

![WP_20181101_20_20_38_Pro](@assets/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/wp_20181101_20_20_38_pro.jpg)

The enclosure was pretty solid and initial attempts to pry it open failed miserably. I then had to use a bit of force 🔨 (fully knowing the risks 🔥 involved and being ultra careful).

![WP\_20181101\_20\_20\_07\_Pro](@assets/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/wp_20181101_20_20_07_pro.jpg)

![WP\_20181101\_20\_26\_09\_Pro](@assets/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/wp_20181101_20_26_09_pro.jpg)

![WP\_20181101\_20\_29\_41\_Pro](@assets/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/wp_20181101_20_29_41_pro.jpg)

Neat stacks of 6 lithium-ion batteries.

![WP\_20181101\_20\_39\_41\_Pro](@assets/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/wp_20181101_20_39_41_pro.jpg)

So the voltage comes to around ~ 3.7 \* 3 =  ~ 11.1 V

![WP\_20181102\_21\_17\_19\_Pro](@assets/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/wp_20181102_21_17_19_pro.jpg)

![WP_20181102_21_18_08_Pro](@assets/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/wp_20181102_21_18_08_pro.jpg)

I plan to use this as a power supply or perhaps make a power bank. These are the [standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_battery_sizes) 18650 type batteries.

I'm planning to charge these using a Li-ion charger I'd [made](/2018/09/20/making-a-li-ion-battery-charger) earlier and see if the batteries are any good.

More updates later.
