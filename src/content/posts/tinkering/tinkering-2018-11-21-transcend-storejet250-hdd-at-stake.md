Another dead HDD. Plug it in, lights up, you can hear the disc spinning and then it stops suddenly with a click.

My brother's pretty sure that he hasn't dropped it. Even if he did, that StoreJet was drop tested according to US military standards (Transcend flaunted this information on the disk's cover).That

=\> Tinker time

- Removed the protective rubber covering.
  ![WP_20180822_15_14_12_Pro](@assets/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/wp_20180822_15_14_12_pro1.jpg)
- Pricked the silver hologram stickers to reveal hidden screws
  ![WP_20180822_15_14_38_Pro](@assets/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/wp_20180822_15_14_38_pro1.jpg)
- Unscrewed the enclosure
  ![WP_20180822_15_15_48_Pro](@assets/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/wp_20180822_15_15_48_pro1.jpg)
- Removed the shock absorbing material
  ![WP_20180822_15_15_58_Pro](@assets/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/wp_20180822_15_15_58_pro1.jpg)
- With the SATA to USB mini adaptor removed
  ![WP_20180822_15_18_11_Pro](@assets/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/wp_20180822_15_18_11_pro1.jpg)
- The HDD
  ![WP_20180822_15_18_23_Pro](@assets/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/wp_20180822_15_18_23_pro1.jpg)
  Okay so Transcend uses Samsung's hard disks. (Fairly common practice in the electronics industry).
