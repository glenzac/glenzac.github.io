## Blogs

This is a list of personal blogs and websites (related to electronics) that I've stumbled upon, over the years. Some of these are well known, most are hidden gems. I've included the main focus of the each website alongside the links. It may not be right in all cases. Also these are not in any particular order.

I might have missed many, so if you know about other such blogs do comment below and I'll add it here.

## **Simulators**

add multisim and other tools

### Analog

- [Electric VLSI Design System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_(software) "Electric (software)"), used to draw schematics and lay out integrated circuits
- [Oregano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregano_(software) "Oregano (software)")
- [SPICE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SPICE "SPICE") and variants, such as [Ngspice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ngspice "Ngspice")

### Digital

- [KTechLab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KTechLab "KTechLab")
- [Logisim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logisim "Logisim")
- [Logic Friday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logic_Friday "Logic Friday")

### Mixed-signal (analog and digital)

- [GNU Circuit Analysis Package](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_Circuit_Analysis_Package "GNU Circuit Analysis Package") (Gnucap)
- [Ngspice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ngspice "Ngspice"), including digital XSPICE
- [Quite Universal Circuit Simulator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quite_Universal_Circuit_Simulator "Quite Universal Circuit Simulator") (Qucs)
- [Xyce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandia_National_Laboratories#Open-source_software "Sandia National Laboratories"), capable of solving extremely large circuit problems from [Sandia National Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandia_National_Laboratories "Sandia National Laboratories")[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_free_electronics_circuit_simulators#cite_note-1)

### Proprietary licenses

- [CPU Sim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CPU_Sim "CPU Sim"), freeware
- [Infineon Designer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TINA_(program) "TINA (program)"), free online, special version of TINAcloud licensed to [Infineon Technologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infineon_Technologies "Infineon Technologies")[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_free_electronics_circuit_simulators#cite_note-2)
- [LTspice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LTspice "LTspice"), freeware, developed by [Linear Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_Technology "Linear Technology") and [Analog Devices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analog_Devices "Analog Devices")
- [Micro-Cap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Micro-Cap "Micro-Cap"), free limited student version
- [PLECS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PLECS "PLECS"), free for some students, developed by [Plexim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plexim "Plexim")
- [SapWin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SapWin "SapWin"), freeware
- [TINA-TI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TINA_(program) "TINA (program)"), freeware, special version of TINA licensed to [Texas Instruments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Instruments "Texas Instruments")[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_free_electronics_circuit_simulators#cite_note-3)
