## Top 10 that I use daily:

**Browser** \- [Mozilla Firefox](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/exp/firefox/)

**Learning / Spaced Repetition** \- [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/)

**Screenshot tool** \- [ShareX](https://getsharex.com/)

**Personal Knowledge base** \- Trilium

- Research - [Zotero](https://www.zotero.org/)
- **PDF Viewer**\- [Sumatra PDF](https://www.sumatrapdfreader.org/free-pdf-reader.html)
- **PDF Editor** \- [LibreOffice Draw](https://www.libreoffice.org/discover/draw/)
- **Password Manager** \- [KeepassXC](https://keepassxc.org/)
- **Image Viewer** \- [Image Glass](https://imageglass.org/)
- **Scripting** \- [AutoHotKey](https://www.autohotkey.com/)
- **Image Compression** \- [Caesium](https://saerasoft.com/caesium/)
- **Image/OS Flashing tool** \- [Balena Etcher](https://www.balena.io/etcher/)
- **e-Book Reader** \- [Calibre](https://calibre-ebook.com/)
- **PCB Designing** (ECAD) - [KiCad](https://kicad-pcb.org/)
- **Archives /** **Zip files** \- [7Zip](https://www.7-zip.org/)
- **Torrenting and P2P** \- [qBittorent](https://www.qbittorrent.org/)
- **Media Player**\- [VLC](https://www.videolan.org/vlc/)
- **Video encoding or conversion** \- [Handbrake](https://handbrake.fr/)
- **Office Suite** \- [Libre office](https://www.libreoffice.org/)
- **Desktop Launcher** \- [Wox](http://www.wox.one/)
- **Desktop Customization** \- [Rainmeter](https://www.rainmeter.net/)
- **Virtualization** \- [Virtual Box](https://www.virtualbox.org/)
- **Calculator** \- [SpeedCrunch](https://www.virtualbox.org/)
- **FTP** \- [FileZilla](https://filezilla-project.org/)
- Development - VSCode
