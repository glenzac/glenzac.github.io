I've been reading quite a lot of non-fiction lately (Well, not really a "lot" according to [Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/user_challenges/34635905)). Many of these books are so good that I started highlighting whole paragraphs or quotes or even small stories that I find interesting.

I've been doing this for a while now and now I'm 13 books down (for this year) and I realized that I don't really remember many of the moving things I read over the year.

So, I've decided to put up posts for each of the books that I've read in the last year. The post will contain all my Highlights and Notes (if any).
