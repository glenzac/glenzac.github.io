title: Tiger
---
Tiger, the vanishing creature,
thanks to hunting and its features.
And why is it done?
for cures,show or fun?

we should have thought earlier
and made decisions,quicker
On seeing it's declining numbers
and act before the population stumbles

why are they treated cruelly?
is it because it's a man-eater?
If food is scarce in the forest, solely
 it would be out for anything,later

All because of man's deeds
their environment destroyed;
thus depriving them, their needs
and so tigers prowl out,
just to be hunted
