title: The Setting Sun
---
The sun neared the horizon
Round, red and glowing,
and all the sky turned orange
proclaiming its glory.

Such a grand farewell;
I couldn't believe,
that it happens everyday
silently, all by itself.

yet it continues
unchanged by time,
to rise again
in all its glory!
