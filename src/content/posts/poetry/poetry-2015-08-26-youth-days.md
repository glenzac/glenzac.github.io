title: Youth days
---
These days are the most joyful days in one's life.
A person shall not forget his past,even in strife.

All those days in colleges and schools
violating all the disciplinary rules.

Sitting with friends and fools
Pulling each other's stools

Oh! How I wish those days would come back
reviewing the old times in my memory pack

Hanging around with pals in a pool,
was thrilling and cool.

Teasing others and cracking jokes,
flattering others and accepting mocks

Violating the rules and going for a cinema.
Getting caught and being put in a dilemma

Oh!How I wish.....
