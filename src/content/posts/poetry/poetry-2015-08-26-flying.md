title: Flying
---
Look at the birds, soar
high in the air

Oh! wish I could be one
then it will be a lot fun

flying through the air
with other pretty birds near

diving and gliding as I move
I'm skilled,to others I prove.

As I move past clouds
I see new worlds.

Oh! wish I could be, one
then it will be a lot, fun.
