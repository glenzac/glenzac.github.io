title: Cheers....(Drinking)...
---
Hey thee who drinks,
beware of that silent killer,
for it will destroy all your links

once you take it in,
during sorrow or grief;
it's like falling into a lion's den
and get killed in very brief.

Oh! this social evil!
which lingers around teens,
luring a person to sin,like the devil
once fallen, there are no escape means.

Everyday there's quarrel in his house.
There isn't love or peace
The man's attitude like a louse
has brought the house to pieces

So thee who drinks
Stop this evil
before it stops you!
